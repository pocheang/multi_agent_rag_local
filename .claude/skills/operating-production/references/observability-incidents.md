# Observability and Incidents

Map critical user journeys to SLIs/SLOs for availability, latency, correctness/quality, freshness, and durability. Define window, exclusions, source, target, and error-budget response.

Use structured logs, metrics, and traces with request/session, tenant-safe, agent, and revision correlation. Dashboards move from user symptoms to service saturation/errors/latency, dependencies, deployments, and AI/RAG quality. Alerts require action, severity, owner, runbook, and deduplication.

During incidents: declare severity/roles, start timeline, preserve evidence, stabilize reversibly, communicate impact/action/next update, verify recovery, then complete a blameless review with corrective owners/dates.

Never log sensitive prompts/documents or use secrets/high-cardinality identifiers as labels.
