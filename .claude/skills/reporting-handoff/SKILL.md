# Reporting and Handoff

Create decision-ready, evidence-linked material for a named audience.

1. Identify artifact type, audience, period, owner, confidentiality, revision/environment, and decision deadline.
2. Lead with outcome/status, impact, variance, risks, decisions, and requested actions.
3. Link source evidence; label facts, forecasts, assumptions, and unknowns.
4. Give every risk/action an owner and date. Remove secrets, personal data, and unnecessary exploit detail.
5. Verify commands, links, ownership, freshness, and that the receiving owner can execute critical procedures.

Read [reports.md](references/reports.md) for status, quality, release, deployment, incident, security, audit, and closure reports. Read [documentation-handoff.md](references/documentation-handoff.md) for architecture docs, runbooks, onboarding, and handoff.

Do not report green status with an unresolved critical blocker or declare handoff complete because a meeting occurred.
